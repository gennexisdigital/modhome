How we work服务流程

From first conversation to final styling.从首次沟通到最终陈设。

A clear, four-stage process — so you always know what happens next, and when.清晰的四个阶段——让您始终了解下一步是什么、何时发生。

1

Discovery需求洞察

A site visit and briefing call. We map your audience, budget and timeline, and reply with a scope and fee proposal within five working days.实地勘察与需求沟通，明确目标客群、预算与时间表，五个工作日内提供设计范围与报价。

2

Concept概念设计

Mood, planning and material direction presented as a single, coherent story — with 3D visuals of the key moments.以完整叙事呈现氛围、平面与材料方向，并提供关键空间的三维效果图。

3

Design development深化设计

Full construction drawings, lighting, joinery and FF&E specifications, coordinated with your contractor and suppliers.完整施工图、灯光、固定家具与软装清单，并与施工方及供应商协调落实。

4

Delivery & styling落地与陈设

Site supervision through handover, then final styling and photography so the space opens exactly as designed.全程驻场监督直至交付，最终完成软装陈设与摄影，让空间如设计般呈现。
